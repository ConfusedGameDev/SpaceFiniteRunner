Two ways to get a track

A runner level (RunnerLevelDefinition) gets its track one of two ways. Which one is decided by a single field, track.

track = empty

Generated every run

The TrackGenerator rolls a new track from its shape and spawn-set assets at the level's trackLengthMeters (0 = GameSettings.trackLengthMeters). Seed 0 means a new layout every run; any other seed repeats exactly.

track = a TrackLayoutAsset

A saved track

The level plays exactly that track: its road, every orb, gate, ramp and coin, its length and, when it sets one, its own countdown. Made with Generate Track and reshaped by hand with Edit Track.

Either way the rest of the game can't tell the difference. The ship, the patrol, the colliders, the HUD and hyperspace all read the same track.

How a run builds its track

The track is handled in two halves that never mix:

  • Deciding. The whole track is decided when the run starts: every knot, sweep, bank, feature and the end, plus every placement as a TrackPlacement record. About 200 knots and 700 records take a fraction of a second. A saved track skips this step: its records are loaded instead. Every random draw happens here.
  • Building. Records become GameObjects only inside the stream window around the ship. Road art, pickups, gates and colliders exist from the ship to aheadDistance ahead, and are culled behindDistance behind. Building draws no random numbers, so the same record always builds the same object.
  • SettledDistance is how far the track has been built. The colliders and the road art stream to it.
  • A restart rebuilds from the start of the track, never from where the ship last was.
  • An edit-mode preview, or a scene without a GameManager, stays an endless preview that decides as it streams.

Make a track, start to finish

  1. Open FiniteRunner_Test and select the Track object (it holds the TrackGenerator). The authoring tools are at the bottom of its inspector.
  2. Click Generate Track. The track is decided in full with the scene's run rules: the level's length, the run-up and the end ramps. The status box shows its length, placement count and seed, and the whole track is built in the scene, end ramps included.
  3. Click Save Track As… and save the asset in Assets/04.Data/FiniteRunner/Tracks/. Replacing an existing track asks first.
  4. Click Set as Current Track. This writes the asset into the scene level's track field.
  5. Press Play. The run loads exactly that track, and the generator's inspector shows saved: <asset>.
  6. To change the track, pick it in Track asset, click Edit Track, work in the Road and Placements tabs, then Save. Stop Editing with unsaved changes asks Save, Discard or Keep Editing.
  7. Optionally, give the asset its own countdown in its inspector (Time Limit Seconds; 0 uses the GameSettings value).
Tip

To compare a saved track with a generated one, turn on FORCE RUNTIME TRACK in the debug menu's CORE SETTINGS and retry. Nothing is written to any asset.

Draw track, Detail radius (500–20 000 m around the Scene camera), Placements and Labels. These are per-user preferences stored in EditorPrefs, not project data.

Editing the road

In the Road tab of Edit Track, click a knot's sphere in the Scene view to select it.

  • Move. The position handle moves the knot. Dragging it up and down changes the grade.
  • Bank. The disc around the knot's forward axis, or the Bank (°) slider. Road bank (°) next to it shows what the road actually leans there. The spline re-leans a sharply bent knot to its curve, so the two can differ by a few degrees.
  • Insert Knot After / Delete Knot / Previous / Frame / Next. Moving or inserting a knot re-aims its neighbours; each keeps its own bank.
  • Spans. Drag the dots at the ends of flat sweeps (orange) and open straights (red) along the road. The selected knot's segment gets buttons to add an Open Straight, add a Flat Sweep (with its outer side), or Remove Spans Here.

After every edit the generator recomputes what the edit moved: every knot's track distance, the length, the start of the final run-up (the same knot as before), the end ramps (one ramp-length before the end) and the run-up's keep-out. Every other placement keeps its distance and slides with the road.

Locked knots

  • Grey: the first knot, and every knot of the final run-up, which stays a straight, walled, level run to the ramps.
  • Cyan: a knot a ramp stands on. It keeps bank 0, its tangent turns with it, and it can't be deleted.
  • On a track with loops or tubes, knot editing is off, because moving road before a section would shift it. Spans can still be edited.

Dragging, saving and undo

  • While a handle is dragged, only the reshaped stretch rebuilds: two knots either side of a moved knot, or the stretch between a span end's old and new spot. That takes about 10 ms a frame. The whole track rebuilds on release, in about 0.4 s for a 92 km track.
  • Nothing is written to disk while editing. The panel shows ● Unsaved changes with Save and Revert.
  • Every change is undoable (Ctrl/Cmd+Z), including Discard and the recomputed distances. An undo also redraws the track on show after editing stops.

Editing placements

The Placements tab of Edit Track works on the track's records: select, move, add, delete and check them.

  • Select a placement by clicking its dot, or from the list of the 25 placements nearest the Scene camera.
  • Move it by dragging the circle along and across the road. It is projected back to a distance and a lateral, and clamped to the lane.
  • Edit its numbers: distance, lateral, height (prefabs) and a variant control for its kind (orb tier, gate type, beam length, rotor speed, wave, catalog power-up, coin value). Or Duplicate (+30 m), Delete or Frame it.
  • Place: choose from Place (catalog power-ups, the four laser gate types, every ramp in the feature table, a coin, or any prefab), press Click in Scene to Place, and click the road. Esc stops.
  • End ramps (they follow the end of the road) and loops (they are their section) are not moved here.

Reroll Placements

Decides every speed orb, repair orb, laser gate and coin afresh, with a new seed, on the road as it stands. Ramps, loops, end ramps, catalog power-ups and custom prefabs stay, and the rerolled items keep clear of them by the generator's usual rules. It asks first and can be undone.

Ramps

A moved or added ramp takes its keep-out with it, so gates and the patrol duel stay clear of the new spot. It does not straighten the road under its landing. A ramp moved onto a curve lands wherever the road goes.

Adding a power-up

The palette's power-ups come from the placement catalog, Assets/04.Data/Resources/FiniteRunner_PlacementCatalog, or from the generator's own Placement Catalog field if one is set. It ships with the Green, Blue and Purple orbs and the Repair orb. Adding a new one needs no code:

  1. Make a PadDefinition for what it does (speed change, orb or flat pad, size) and a prefab for how it looks.
  2. Add an entry to the catalog and set Built As to Pickup.
  3. Fill its Pad: prefab, definition, boost multiplier, colour, sway, spin and lane. Give it a Display Name and a Color for the palette and the gizmos.
  4. It now appears under Place → Power-up/…, and is built and streamed like any orb.
Keep the order

Pickup records point at catalog entries by index. Add new entries at the end; reordering or removing entries changes what saved tracks build.

Reading the Scene view

The generator draws the track whenever it's on show: after Generate Track, after Preview Saved Track, or during a run. It draws from the live track data, never from GameObjects. The whole centre line is always drawn; detail appears within the detail radius of the Scene camera. The drawing data is rebuilt once per track change (about 40 ms for 70 km); repaints only draw.

Placements are marked in their spawner's colour (orb tiers use their own): ramps as their footprint, laser gates as their beam (rotors with their swept circle), repair orbs as a double ring, coins as small cubes.

The chase minimap

The minimap draws the whole track from above in the hand-placed TrackMap rectangle of ChaseMinimap.prefab. The start is at the bottom, the finish at the top, and the aspect ratio is kept.

  • Ship and patrol are dots at their real distances along the line. The patrol sits at the ship's distance minus its gap, and hides whenever it's hidden from the map.
  • The flown stretch is tinted.
  • Markers for every ramp, laser gate and the finish. Orbs are never marked.
  • The map redraws whenever the track changes: a restart, or hyperspace bringing the end in.
  • The old vertical strip's bar and icons are hidden by default (showStrip). Its distance labels always stay.

Layout is the designer's. Place and size the TrackMap rectangle by hand in the prefab. Rebuild UI creates it, and anything else unwired, in a default spot; in edit mode it shows a sample curve so you can judge the look. Everything else lives in ChaseMinimapSettings under Track map: showTrackMap, showStrip, mapStartAtBottom, mapPadding, mapSamples, mapLineWidth, mapLineColor, mapDrivenColor, mapBackground, markerSize, and the ramp, gate and finish marker colours and sprites.

Rules for changing the code

Breaking one of these doesn't throw an error; the track just stops repeating or stops loading exactly.

  • Random draws happen only while deciding, and the road's own stream is drawn only in road order (segments, sweeps, features). Anything else draws from a stream of its own, hashed off the layout seed: coins, ramp laterals, each spawner.
  • Building draws nothing. A new placement kind rolls everything in its decide step and stores the rolls in the record.
  • Placement is staged. Laser gates go first, each pickup spawner stops one pad length short of the stage before it, and coins another pad length short, placing only whole rows. That way, whatever a placement keeps off is always decided before it.
  • Save knots as they were handed in (TrackManager.AppendedKnots), never the spline's final knots. AutoSmooth re-leans knots when neighbours arrive, and saving the final ones leans them twice on load.
  • Knots are removed only past built road (the hyperspace cut-back). Removing knots reshapes the segment ending at the last kept knot.
  • Caches along the track key on TrackManager.Revision, never on its length. A new track can be as long as the old one.
  • Gameplay never writes a track asset. The generator copies its records; the editor writes the asset with Undo.
  • Distance from the track start is the coordinate. Placements keep their distance when the road changes shape.
  • Don't edit scripts while in play mode. Domain reload is off, and a mid-play recompile wipes the generator's in-memory state.

Known limitations

  • A loop's or tube's rolled parameters can't be edited, and knots can't be edited on a track that has loops or tubes. Both are at 0 % in the shipped shape.
  • No tangent handles on feature knots.
  • A moved ramp doesn't straighten the road under its landing; the checks flag only a landing that reaches the run-up.
  • The generator is the only streamer. There's no Contracts interface between it and what it feeds (PRD R6.3).
  • Pickup records index the placement catalog, so reordering catalog entries changes saved tracks.
